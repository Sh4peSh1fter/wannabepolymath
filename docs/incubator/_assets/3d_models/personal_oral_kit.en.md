this is my personal oral kit that holds all the things that are related to my oral care routine. 

the things it wil l hold:
1. toothbrush
2. toothbrush head
3. morning toothpaste
4. evening toothpaste
5. floss string
6. tounge scraper
7. (optional) water flosser 

## Guiding Goals

1. I dont want any part that touches my mouth to be in contact with the plastic directly.
2. the holder should allow an optimal way to stay dry, as most of the tools will be wet after use. 
3. portable, meaning I can easly move it from my bedroom or wall shelf to the bathroom with me.
