# About the "Wanna be Polymath" Project

Welcome to the "About" section for the Wanna be Polymath project! This space is dedicated to explaining the foundational pillars upon which this project is built. Whether you're a curious visitor, a potential contributor, or a future polymath yourself, these documents aim to provide clarity on our mission, methods, and the way we do things around here.

Our goal is to be transparent and thorough, creating a shared understanding that helps our community thrive and our knowledge base grow in a thoughtful and organized manner.

## What You'll Find Here:

This section houses the key documents that define and guide the project. Think of it as the project's DNA, open for all to see and understand.

*   **[Vision](./vision.md):** Delve into the core purpose, guiding principles, and aspirational goals that drive the Wanna be Polymath project. Understand *why* we're building this and what we hope to achieve.

*   **[Structure & Design](./structure_design.md):** Explore how the project is organized, from its navigation and content hierarchy to the design philosophy that ensures a user-friendly and flexible platform. This document details *how* we arrange our knowledge.

*   **[Inspiration](./taking_inspiration.md):** Discover the projects, platforms, and ideas that have inspired our approach. Learn about the research and case studies that have informed our design and structural decisions.

*   **[Technology Stack](./tech_stack.md):** Get acquainted with the technologies that power our platform, including our choices for static site generation, hosting, and other tools. Understand the *what* behind our technical infrastructure.

*   **[Style Guide](./style.md):** Learn about our visual identity, including the "Curious Sheep" theme, color palettes, typography, and imagery guidelines that give the project its unique look and feel.

*   **[Accessibility Guidelines](./accessibility.md):** Understand our commitment to making the platform accessible to everyone, covering language support, disability considerations, and technical standards.

*   **[Distribution Strategy](./distribution.md):** Find out how we plan to share our content and reach a wider audience across various platforms and channels.

*   **[Engagement & Contribution](./engagement_and_contribution.md):** Learn how we foster community, manage contributions, and ensure a welcoming and collaborative environment.

*   **[Project Standards](./standards.md):** Review the guidelines for file organization, naming conventions, documentation practices, and content quality that help maintain consistency and excellence.

*   **[Formatting Guide](./formatting_guide/index.md):** (Work in Progress) A detailed guide on how to format content using Markdown and specific platform features to ensure consistency and readability.

We encourage you to explore these documents to get a comprehensive understanding of the Wanna be Polymath project. Your feedback and contributions are always welcome as we continue to build and refine this exciting endeavor!
