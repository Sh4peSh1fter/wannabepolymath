# Welcome to the Wanna be Polymath project

Hey :)  

I'm learning new stuff everyday and I want to share some of them in my own unique way.  
the goal is simple. I want to be a polymath, I want to document some stuff that I write, and I want you to see my journey and get inspired.  

## Getting Started

The site is organized into several "Corners," each with a unique purpose. Don't worry about understanding everything at once — start with a section that interests you, and you'll see how it all connects as you explore.

If you're inspired to create your own digital garden, you can follow my guide [here](./about/setting_up_your_own_website.md).

## Structure

The Wanna be Polymath site is organized into several key areas, each designed for a specific purpose in our shared journey of learning:

1.  **Home (`docs/index.md`)**
    *   You are here! This is the main landing page.
    *   It provides an overview of the "Wanna be Polymath" project, my vision, how to navigate the platform, and how you can use it or contribute.

2.  **Thematic "Corners":** These are the primary sections where content lives, each with a unique focus and style. All content across these Corners will be tagged with relevant topics to aid discovery.
    *   **Round Table (`docs/round_table/`)**: Our collaborative space for dissecting challenges, brainstorming innovative solutions, and conceptualizing projects.
    *   **Incubator (`docs/incubator/`)**: The workshop for active creation – showcasing projects and fledgling ideas being nurtured and developed.
    *   **Academy (`docs/academy/`)**: The learning and skill-sharing hub, featuring tutorials, how-to guides, troubleshooting journals, and in-depth articles I write.
    *   **Observatory (`docs/observatory/`)**: Where we map the constellations of knowledge. It's a space to collect, define, and connect key terms, concepts, influential figures, and organizations. This section also serves as our primary hub for exploring specific **Topics** in-depth. You'll find aggregated information and links to topic-related content from all other Corners here.
    *   **Bazaar (`docs/bazaar/`)**: My personal research corner for finding the best tools and items to fit specific needs. Think of it as a detailed shopping list where I document my process for choosing everything from kitchenware to tech gadgets, focusing on quality, health, and value.

3.  **About (`docs/about/`)**
    *   This section offers a glimpse into the "why" and "how" of the Wanna be Polymath project itself.
    *   You'll find my project's Vision, this Structure & Design explanation, technical details, content standards, and my Formatting Guide.
