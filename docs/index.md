## Getting Started

I divided the website into different sections, each with its own unique style and purpose.

### Structure

The Wanna be Polymath site is organized into several key areas, each designed for a specific purpose in our shared journey of learning and creation:

1.  **Home (`docs/index.md`)**
    *   You are here! This is the main landing page.
    *   It provides an overview of the "Wanna be Polymath" project, our vision, how to navigate the platform, and how you can contribute.

2.  **Thematic "Corners":** These are the primary sections where content lives, each with a unique focus and style. All content across these Corners will be tagged with relevant topics to aid discovery.
    *   **Round Table (`docs/round_table/`)**: Our collaborative space for dissecting challenges, brainstorming innovative solutions, and conceptualizing projects.
    *   **Incubator (`docs/incubator/`)**: The workshop for active creation – showcasing projects and fledgling ideas being nurtured and developed.
    *   **Academy (`docs/academy/`)**: The learning and skill-sharing hub, featuring tutorials, how-to guides, troubleshooting journals, and in-depth articles.
    *   **Observatory (`docs/observatory/`)**: Where we map the constellations of knowledge. It's a space to collect, define, and connect key terms, concepts, influential figures, and organizations. This section also serves as our primary hub for exploring specific **Topics** in-depth. You'll find aggregated information and links to topic-related content from all other Corners here.
    *   **Bazaar (`docs/bazaar/`)**: A vibrant marketplace of intriguing tidbits! This is where we share fascinating, small, and often random day-to-day facts, discoveries, and insights.

3.  **About (`docs/about/`)**
    *   This section offers a glimpse into the "why" and "how" of the Wanna be Polymath project itself.
    *   You'll find our project's Vision, this Structure & Design explanation, technical details, content standards, and our Formatting Guide.

**How It All Connects:**

While the "Corners" give thematic context, all content will be tagged with specific **Topics**. The **Observatory** will play a key role in organizing and providing entry points into these topics, linking to relevant materials across all other Corners. This approach allows you to explore, for example, everything related to "Artificial Intelligence" whether it's a tutorial in the Academy, a project in the Incubator, or a discussion in the Round Table, with the Observatory acting as a central guide for that topic.

Our goal is a flexible yet organized system that encourages both focused learning within a topic (often starting or centered in the Observatory) and serendipitous discovery across different areas of knowledge!
