# Personal Hygiene

Welcome to the Personal Hygiene corner. This section is dedicated to researching and selecting the best products for daily health and cleanliness.

The goal is to move beyond marketing claims and understand the science and materials behind the items we use on our bodies every day. Each entry will be a detailed investigation into a product category, documenting the process of defining personal needs, researching the options, and choosing items that are both effective and safe.

## Defining 'Healthy' in Hygiene Products

When researching these items, I'll be focusing on a few key aspects to determine what "healthy" and "effective" really mean:

*   **Material & Ingredient Safety:** What is the product made of? This includes avoiding known irritants or harmful chemicals (e.g., certain sulfates, parabens) and choosing safe materials for tools (e.g., BPA-free plastics, quality bristles).
*   **Proven Effectiveness:** Does the product work as intended according to scientific evidence or trusted dental/dermatological standards, not just marketing? (e.g., ADA Seal of Acceptance for dental products).
*   **Gentleness & Body-Compatibility:** Does the product support the body's natural state without causing harm? This means avoiding things that are too abrasive (damaging tooth enamel or skin) or too harsh (stripping natural oils).
*   **Durability & Sustainability:** For tools, is it built to last? For consumables, are there sustainable options available?
