# Bazaar

Welcome to the Bazaar! This is my personal research corner for finding the best tools, gadgets, and items to fit specific needs.

Instead of just a list of things to buy, each entry here is a deep dive into a specific item category. I document my process of defining needs, researching the options, and comparing products to make an informed decision. Think of it as a transparent shopping list, designed to help you understand the "why" behind every choice, focusing on quality, health, and long-term value.
