*   size: around 230 cm x 160 cm

1. Polypropylene (Olefin)
Pros:

Very stain-resistant and durable.

Doesn’t absorb moisture easily, making it resistant to mildew.

Can often be cleaned with a broom, vacuum, or hose.

Cons:

Not as soft or natural-looking as wool or cotton.

2. Nylon
Pros:

Excellent stain and dirt resistance.

Very durable and resilient.

Easy to clean with a vacuum or mild soap.

Cons:

Slightly more expensive than polypropylene.

3. Flatweave Cotton or Synthetic Blends
Pros:

Very thin and lightweight — easy to shake out or machine wash (if size allows).

Low pile = minimal dust trapping.

Cons:

Less plush underfoot.

May require a rug pad to stay in place.
