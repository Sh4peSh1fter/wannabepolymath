# Chemistry

## Introduction

Chemistry is the study of matter — its composition, structure, and properties, and the changes
it undergoes during reactions. Often called the "central science," it sits between physics and
the life sciences: the atomic and bonding models it borrows from physics become, in its hands,
the vocabulary for explaining materials, medicines, and living cells. A newcomer's first
anchors are the **atom** and its structure, the **chemical bond**, the **periodic table** that
organizes the elements, and the **reactions** that transform them. This page is a Map of
Content: the definitions live in `keywords`, the people and bodies in `entities`, and the
reading in `sources`.
