# Key Entities — Chemistry

Key people, organizations, and institutions that shaped chemistry. Entries are neutral and
descriptive — what they are and why they matter.

## Key Figures

- **Antoine Lavoisier**
    - *Also known as*: Antoine-Laurent de Lavoisier
    - *Description*: French chemist often called the "father of modern chemistry"; formulated the law of conservation of mass and helped establish the modern system of chemical nomenclature.
    - *Known for*: [Reactions & Stoichiometry](./keywords.en.md#reactions-stoichiometry)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Antoine_Lavoisier)

- **John Dalton**
    - *Description*: English chemist and physicist who introduced the modern atomic theory of matter into chemistry.
    - *Known for*: [Atomic Structure](./keywords.en.md#atomic-structure)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Dalton)

- **Dmitri Mendeleev**
    - *Also known as*: Dmitri Ivanovich Mendeleev
    - *Description*: Russian chemist who formulated the periodic law and published a periodic table (1869) that predicted the properties of then-undiscovered elements.
    - *Known for*: [The Periodic Table](./keywords.en.md#the-periodic-table)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dmitri_Mendeleev)

- **Marie Curie**
    - *Also known as*: Maria Skłodowska-Curie
    - *Description*: Polish-French physicist and chemist who pioneered research on radioactivity, discovered the elements polonium and radium, and won Nobel Prizes in both physics and chemistry.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marie_Curie)

- **Linus Pauling**
    - *Description*: American chemist whose work on the nature of the chemical bond and the concept of electronegativity shaped modern structural chemistry.
    - *Known for*: [Chemical Bonding](./keywords.en.md#chemical-bonding)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linus_Pauling)

## Organizations & Companies

- **International Union of Pure and Applied Chemistry**
    - *Also known as*: IUPAC
    - *Description*: International body that sets standards for chemical nomenclature, terminology, and measurement.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Union_of_Pure_and_Applied_Chemistry)

- **American Chemical Society**
    - *Also known as*: ACS
    - *Description*: Scientific society dedicated to advancing chemistry, and a major publisher of peer-reviewed chemistry journals.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Chemical_Society)

- **Royal Society of Chemistry**
    - *Also known as*: RSC
    - *Description*: UK-based professional body for chemical scientists and a leading publisher of chemistry research and reference works.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Society_of_Chemistry)
