# Sources & Further Reading — Biology

A curated list of resources for diving deeper into biology.

## Websites & Online Publications

1. National Center for Biotechnology Information (NCBI)
    - *Link*: [ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/)
    - *Note*: A hub of biomedical and genomic databases, including PubMed and GenBank.
2. Khan Academy — Biology
    - *Link*: [khanacademy.org/science/biology](https://www.khanacademy.org/science/biology)
    - *Note*: Free courses spanning cell biology, genetics, evolution, and ecology.
3. Nature
    - *Link*: [nature.com](https://www.nature.com/)
    - *Note*: A leading multidisciplinary science publisher with extensive coverage of the life sciences.

## Videos & Documentaries

1. Crash Course Biology
    - *Link*: [youtube.com/playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PL3EED4C1D684D3ADF)
    - *Note*: An accessible introductory series covering the core topics of biology.

## Academic Papers & Journals

1. *Cell*
    - *Link*: [cell.com](https://www.cell.com/)
    - *Note*: A flagship peer-reviewed journal for research across molecular and cell biology.

## Books

1. *On the Origin of Species* — by Charles Darwin
    - *Note*: The foundational work introducing evolution by natural selection.
2. *The Selfish Gene* — by Richard Dawkins
    - *Note*: An influential gene-centered view of evolution written for a general audience.
3. *Molecular Biology of the Cell* — by Bruce Alberts et al.
    - *Note*: A comprehensive reference textbook on cell and molecular biology.
