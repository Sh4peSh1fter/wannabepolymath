# Key Entities — Biology

Key people, organizations, and institutions that shaped biology. Entries are neutral and
descriptive — what they are and why they matter.

## Key Figures

- **Charles Darwin**
    - *Description*: English naturalist whose theory of evolution by natural selection, set out in *On the Origin of Species*, became the unifying framework of modern biology.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Darwin)

- **Gregor Mendel**
    - *Description*: Moravian friar and scientist whose experiments on pea plants established the foundational laws of heredity, making him the founder of genetics.
    - *Known for*: [Genetics & genomics](./keywords.en.md#biotechnology-genomics)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gregor_Mendel)

- **Carl Linnaeus**
    - *Also known as*: Carl von Linné
    - *Description*: Swedish botanist who formalized binomial nomenclature, the modern system for naming and classifying living organisms.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Linnaeus)

- **Louis Pasteur**
    - *Description*: French chemist and microbiologist whose work on germ theory, vaccination, and pasteurization transformed medicine and microbiology.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Louis_Pasteur)

- **Rosalind Franklin**
    - *Description*: British chemist whose X-ray diffraction images of DNA were critical to determining its double-helix structure.
    - *Known for*: [Genetics & genomics](./keywords.en.md#biotechnology-genomics)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rosalind_Franklin)

- **James Watson**
    - *Description*: American molecular biologist who, with Francis Crick, proposed the double-helix model of DNA in 1953.
    - *Known for*: [Genetics & genomics](./keywords.en.md#biotechnology-genomics)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Watson)

- **Francis Crick**
    - *Description*: British molecular biologist and co-discoverer of the structure of DNA who later articulated the central dogma of molecular biology.
    - *Known for*: [Genetics & genomics](./keywords.en.md#biotechnology-genomics)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Francis_Crick)

- **Lynn Margulis**
    - *Description*: American evolutionary biologist who advanced the endosymbiotic theory explaining the origin of eukaryotic organelles such as mitochondria.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lynn_Margulis)

## Organizations & Companies

- **National Institutes of Health**
    - *Also known as*: NIH
    - *Description*: The primary agency of the United States government responsible for biomedical and public health research.
    - *Link*: [nih.gov](https://www.nih.gov)

- **Illumina**
    - *Description*: American biotechnology company that develops and manufactures widely used DNA sequencing and array-based technologies.
    - *Known for*: [Genetics & genomics](./keywords.en.md#biotechnology-genomics)
    - *Link*: [illumina.com](https://www.illumina.com)

## Academic & Research Institutions

- **Wellcome Sanger Institute**
    - *Description*: British genomics and genetics research institute, a major contributor to the Human Genome Project.
    - *Known for*: [Genetics & genomics](./keywords.en.md#biotechnology-genomics)
    - *Link*: [sanger.ac.uk](https://www.sanger.ac.uk)

- **Cold Spring Harbor Laboratory**
    - *Description*: American non-profit research institution focused on molecular biology, genetics, cancer, and neuroscience.
    - *Link*: [cshl.edu](https://www.cshl.edu)

- **European Molecular Biology Laboratory**
    - *Also known as*: EMBL
    - *Description*: Intergovernmental research organization dedicated to the study of molecular biology and bioinformatics across Europe.
    - *Link*: [embl.org](https://www.embl.org)
