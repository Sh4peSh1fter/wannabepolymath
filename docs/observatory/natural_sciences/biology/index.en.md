# Biology

## Introduction

Biology is the scientific study of life. It is a natural science with a broad scope, unified by
several recurring themes: all organisms are made of cells, they process hereditary information
encoded in genes, and they evolve over time through natural selection. From molecules and cells
up to whole ecosystems, biology draws on chemistry for its mechanisms and feeds directly into
medicine, ecology, and biotechnology. This field is organized in three views: the working
**vocabulary**, the **people and institutions** behind it, and where to **read further**.

## Related concepts & fields

- **[Chemistry](../chemistry/index.en.md)** — biochemistry sits at the boundary of the two
  fields; the reactions of proteins, DNA, and metabolism are chemistry applied to living
  systems.
- **[Medicine](../../applied_sciences/medicine/index.en.md)** — applies biological knowledge of
  physiology, genetics, and microbiology to human health and disease.
- **[Earth Science](../earth_science/index.en.md)** — ecology and evolution unfold against the
  backdrop of climate, geology, and the deep-time history of the planet.
