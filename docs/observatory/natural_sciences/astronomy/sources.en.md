# Sources & Further Reading — Astronomy

A curated list of resources for diving deeper into astronomy.

## Websites & Online Publications

1. Astronomy Picture of the Day
    - *Link*: [apod.nasa.gov](https://apod.nasa.gov/apod/)
    - *Note*: A NASA-run archive featuring a new astronomical image each day with a short expert explanation.
2. NASA
    - *Link*: [nasa.gov](https://www.nasa.gov/)
    - *Note*: News, mission data, and educational material spanning the agency's astronomy and space-science programs.
3. ESA/Hubble
    - *Link*: [esahubble.org](https://esahubble.org/)
    - *Note*: The European home of Hubble Space Telescope imagery, news, and science results.

## Videos & Documentaries

1. *Cosmos: A Personal Voyage*
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmos:_A_Personal_Voyage)
    - *Note*: Carl Sagan's landmark 1980 series introducing astronomy and cosmology to a general audience.
2. *Cosmos: A Spacetime Odyssey*
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cosmos:_A_Spacetime_Odyssey)
    - *Note*: The 2014 successor hosted by Neil deGrasse Tyson, covering modern astronomy and the history of science.

## Academic Papers & Journals

1. The Astrophysical Journal
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Astrophysical_Journal)
    - *Note*: A leading peer-reviewed journal publishing research across astronomy and astrophysics.
2. NASA Astrophysics Data System
    - *Link*: [ui.adsabs.harvard.edu](https://ui.adsabs.harvard.edu/)
    - *Note*: A digital library and search portal indexing millions of astronomy and physics publications.

## Books

1. *Cosmos* — by Carl Sagan
    - *Note*: A wide-ranging tour of astronomy, cosmology, and the history of science for general readers.
2. *A Brief History of Time* — by Stephen Hawking
    - *Note*: An accessible introduction to cosmology, black holes, and the origin of the universe.
3. *Pale Blue Dot: A Vision of the Human Future in Space* — by Carl Sagan
    - *Note*: A reflection on humanity's place in the cosmos and the future of space exploration.
