# Key Entities — Astronomy

Key people, organizations, and institutions that shaped astronomy. Entries are neutral and
descriptive — what they are and why they matter.

## Key Figures

- **Nicolaus Copernicus**
    - *Description*: Renaissance mathematician and astronomer who formulated the heliocentric model placing the Sun, rather than the Earth, at the centre of the universe.
    - *Known for*: [Celestial mechanics](./keywords.en.md#celestial-mechanics)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nicolaus_Copernicus)

- **Galileo Galilei**
    - *Description*: Italian astronomer and physicist whose telescopic observations of the Moon, Jupiter's moons, and the phases of Venus supported the heliocentric model.
    - *Known for*: [Observational astronomy](./keywords.en.md#observational-astronomy)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Galileo_Galilei)

- **Johannes Kepler**
    - *Description*: German astronomer and mathematician who derived the three laws of planetary motion describing the elliptical orbits of planets.
    - *Known for*: [Kepler's laws of planetary motion](./keywords.en.md#celestial-mechanics)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Johannes_Kepler)

- **Edwin Hubble**
    - *Description*: American astronomer who showed that galaxies lie far beyond the Milky Way and that their recession speed rises with distance.
    - *Known for*: [Hubble's law](./keywords.en.md#cosmology)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edwin_Hubble)

- **Vera Rubin**
    - *Description*: American astronomer whose measurements of galaxy rotation curves provided strong observational evidence for dark matter.
    - *Known for*: [Dark matter](./keywords.en.md#galaxies-deep-sky-objects)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vera_Rubin)

- **Cecilia Payne-Gaposchkin**
    - *Description*: British-American astronomer who first showed that stars are composed chiefly of hydrogen and helium.
    - *Known for*: [Stars & stellar evolution](./keywords.en.md#stars-stellar-evolution)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cecilia_Payne-Gaposchkin)

## Influencers

- **Carl Sagan**
    - *Description*: American astronomer and science communicator whose books and television series brought astronomy to a broad public audience.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Sagan)

- **Neil deGrasse Tyson**
    - *Description*: American astrophysicist and prominent science communicator, host of the *Cosmos: A Spacetime Odyssey* series.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Neil_deGrasse_Tyson)

## Organizations & Companies

- **NASA**
    - *Also known as*: National Aeronautics and Space Administration
    - *Description*: The United States federal agency responsible for the civilian space program and for aeronautics and space research.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NASA)

- **European Space Agency**
    - *Also known as*: ESA
    - *Description*: Intergovernmental organization of European states dedicated to the exploration of space.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Space_Agency)

- **International Astronomical Union**
    - *Also known as*: IAU
    - *Description*: The international body of professional astronomers, responsible for the official naming and classification of celestial bodies.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/International_Astronomical_Union)

## Academic & Research Institutions

- **European Southern Observatory**
    - *Also known as*: ESO
    - *Description*: Intergovernmental research organization operating major ground-based observatories in Chile, including the Very Large Telescope.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/European_Southern_Observatory)

- **Space Telescope Science Institute**
    - *Also known as*: STScI
    - *Description*: The science operations centre for the Hubble Space Telescope and the James Webb Space Telescope.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Space_Telescope_Science_Institute)
