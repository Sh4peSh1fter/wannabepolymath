# Physics

## Introduction

Physics is the natural science that studies matter, its fundamental constituents, its motion
and behavior through space and time, and the related entities of energy and force. It is among
the most fundamental scientific disciplines: its laws underpin chemistry, astronomy, and the
engineering sciences, and its questions range from the mechanics of everyday objects to the
behavior of subatomic particles and the structure of the cosmos. This field is organized in
three views: the working **vocabulary**, the **people and institutions** behind it, and where
to **read further**.

## Related concepts & fields

- **[Mathematics](../../formal_sciences/mathematics/index.en.md)** — the language of physics;
  calculus, differential equations, and linear algebra are the everyday tools of physical
  theory.
- **[Chemistry](../chemistry/index.en.md)** — governed by physics at the atomic and molecular
  scale; quantum mechanics and thermodynamics bridge the two fields.
- **[Astronomy](../astronomy/index.en.md)** — applies gravitation, relativity, and radiation
  physics to stars, galaxies, and the universe as a whole.
- **[Biology](../biology/index.en.md)** — biophysics applies mechanics, thermodynamics, and
  optics to living systems.
