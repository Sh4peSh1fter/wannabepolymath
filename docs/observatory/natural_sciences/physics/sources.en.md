# Sources & Further Reading — Physics

A curated list of resources for diving deeper into physics.

## Websites & Online Publications

1. HyperPhysics
    - *Link*: [hyperphysics.phy-astr.gsu.edu](http://hyperphysics.phy-astr.gsu.edu/hbase/index.html)
    - *Note*: A concept-map-based reference covering the breadth of introductory and intermediate physics.
2. Khan Academy — Physics
    - *Link*: [khanacademy.org/science/physics](https://www.khanacademy.org/science/physics)
    - *Note*: Free courses spanning mechanics, electromagnetism, and modern physics.
3. MIT OpenCourseWare — Physics
    - *Link*: [ocw.mit.edu](https://ocw.mit.edu/search/?d=Physics)
    - *Note*: Full university-level physics courses, including lecture notes, problem sets, and video lectures.

## Videos & Documentaries

1. PBS Space Time
    - *Link*: [youtube.com/@pbsspacetime](https://www.youtube.com/@pbsspacetime)
    - *Note*: In-depth explorations of astrophysics, relativity, and quantum theory.
2. Veritasium
    - *Link*: [youtube.com/@veritasium](https://www.youtube.com/@veritasium)
    - *Note*: Science videos that build physical intuition through experiments and clear explanation.

## Academic Papers & Journals

1. Physical Review Letters
    - *Link*: [journals.aps.org/prl](https://journals.aps.org/prl/)
    - *Note*: A leading peer-reviewed journal publishing short, high-impact physics research.
2. arXiv — Physics
    - *Link*: [arxiv.org/archive/physics](https://arxiv.org/archive/physics)
    - *Note*: Open-access preprint repository covering all areas of physics.

## Books

1. *The Feynman Lectures on Physics* — by Richard Feynman, Robert Leighton, and Matthew Sands
    - *Link*: [feynmanlectures.caltech.edu](https://www.feynmanlectures.caltech.edu/)
    - *Note*: A celebrated introduction to physics, available free online.
2. *A Brief History of Time* — by Stephen Hawking
    - *Note*: A popular-science account of cosmology, black holes, and the nature of time.
