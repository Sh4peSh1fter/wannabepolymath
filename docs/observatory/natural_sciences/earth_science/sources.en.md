# Sources & Further Reading — Earth Science

A curated list of resources for diving deeper into Earth science.

## Websites & Online Publications

1. USGS
    - *Link*: [usgs.gov](https://www.usgs.gov)
    - *Note*: The U.S. Geological Survey's data, maps, and real-time monitoring for geology, water, and earthquake hazards.
2. NASA Earth Observatory
    - *Link*: [earthobservatory.nasa.gov](https://earthobservatory.nasa.gov)
    - *Note*: Satellite imagery and plain-language articles on Earth's climate, atmosphere, oceans, and land.
3. NOAA
    - *Link*: [noaa.gov](https://www.noaa.gov)
    - *Note*: Authoritative resources on weather, oceans, and atmospheric science.

## Academic Papers & Journals

1. IPCC Assessment Reports
    - *Link*: [ipcc.ch/reports](https://www.ipcc.ch/reports/)
    - *Note*: Periodic, peer-reviewed syntheses of the scientific understanding of climate change.

## Books

1. *Annals of the Former World* — by John McPhee
    - *Note*: A Pulitzer Prize-winning account of North American geology across deep time.
2. *The Map That Changed the World* — by Simon Winchester
    - *Note*: The story of William Smith and the first modern geological map.
3. *Principles of Geology* — by Charles Lyell
    - *Note*: The foundational 19th-century work that established uniformitarianism.
