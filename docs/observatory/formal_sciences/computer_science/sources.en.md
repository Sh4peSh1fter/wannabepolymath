# Sources & Further Reading — Computer Science

A curated list of resources for diving deeper into computer science. Every entry names the
resource, links it, and says in one line why it earns a place here.

## Websites & Online Publications

1. The Odin Project
    - *Link*: [theodinproject.com](https://www.theodinproject.com/)
    - *Note*: A free, open-source full-stack web development curriculum built around projects.
2. MIT OpenCourseWare
    - *Link*: [ocw.mit.edu](https://ocw.mit.edu/)
    - *Note*: Free lecture notes, assignments, and exams from MIT's computer science courses.
3. GeeksforGeeks
    - *Link*: [geeksforgeeks.org](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/)
    - *Note*: A broad reference for algorithms, data structures, and language-specific tutorials.
4. Stack Overflow
    - *Link*: [stackoverflow.com](https://stackoverflow.com/)
    - *Note*: The largest question-and-answer community for practical programming problems.
5. Coursetro
    - *Link*: [coursetro.com](https://coursetro.com/)
    - *Note*: Courses on UI/UX design and front-end development.
6. PyTorch Tutorials
    - *Link*: [pytorch.org/tutorials](https://pytorch.org/tutorials/)
    - *Note*: Official tutorials for the PyTorch machine learning library.
7. Harvard Online Learning — Data Science: Machine Learning
    - *Link*: [online-learning.harvard.edu](https://online-learning.harvard.edu/course/data-science-machine-learning)
    - *Note*: An online machine-learning course from Harvard University.
8. SearchSecurity (TechTarget)
    - *Link*: [techtarget.com/searchsecurity](https://www.techtarget.com/searchsecurity/)
    - *Note*: Cybersecurity news, articles, and analysis.
9. The Malware Wiki
    - *Link*: [malwiki.org](https://malwiki.org/)
    - *Note*: A wiki dedicated to information about malware.
10. Exploit Database
    - *Link*: [exploit-db.com](https://www.exploit-db.com/)
    - *Note*: A CVE-compliant archive of public exploits and corresponding vulnerable software.
11. Google Hacking Database
    - *Link*: [exploit-db.com/google-hacking-database](https://www.exploit-db.com/google-hacking-database)
    - *Note*: A database of Google dorks for finding vulnerable systems.
12. The Art of Hacking (h4cker)
    - *Link*: [github.com/The-Art-of-Hacking/h4cker](https://github.com/The-Art-of-Hacking/h4cker/tree/master/recon)
    - *Note*: A large GitHub collection of hacking and reconnaissance resources.
13. Phrack
    - *Link*: [phrack.org](http://www.phrack.org/)
    - *Note*: A long-running ezine written by and for hackers.
14. ESET Online Scanner
    - *Link*: [eset.com](https://www.eset.com/int/home/online-scanner/)
    - *Note*: A free online virus scanner.
15. Check Point Threat Map
    - *Link*: [threatmap.checkpoint.com](https://threatmap.checkpoint.com/)
    - *Note*: A real-time map of cyberattacks.
16. Incapsula — Border Gateway Protocol (BGP)
    - *Link*: [imperva.com](https://www.incapsula.com/ddos/border-gateway-protocol-bgp.html)
    - *Note*: An article explaining the Border Gateway Protocol.
17. IsraelClouds — Types of Cyber Attacks
    - *Link*: [israelclouds.com](https://www.israelclouds.com/article/types-cyber-attacks)
    - *Note*: An article on different types of cyber attacks (Hebrew).
18. odi-x — File Whitening

19. CyberSafe
    - *Link*: [cybersafe.co.il](https://cybersafe.co.il/)

21. Awesome Virtualization

    - *Link*: [cs50.harvard.edu](https://cs50.harvard.edu/)
    - *Note*: A rigorous, widely followed introductory course spanning C, Python, SQL, and web development.
2. Computerphile
    - *Link*: [youtube.com/user/Computerphile](https://www.youtube.com/user/Computerphile)
    - *Note*: Short videos explaining computing concepts, often featuring university researchers.

## Academic Papers & Journals

1. Communications of the ACM
    - *Link*: [cacm.acm.org](https://cacm.acm.org/)
    - *Note*: The flagship magazine of the ACM, covering research and practice across computing.

## Books

1. *Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs* — by Harold Abelson and Gerald Jay Sussman
    - *Note*: A classic introduction to programming and abstraction, long used in MIT's core curriculum.
2. *Introduction to Algorithms* — by Thomas H. Cormen, Charles E. Leiserson, Ronald L. Rivest, and Clifford Stein
    - *Note*: The standard comprehensive reference on algorithms and data structures ("CLRS").
3. *The Art of Computer Programming* — by Donald Knuth
    - *Note*: A multi-volume, deeply rigorous treatment of fundamental algorithms.
4. *The Pragmatic Programmer* — by Andrew Hunt and David Thomas
    - *Note*: A practical guide to software craftsmanship and professional development habits.
5. *DevOps Automation with Terraform and VMware*
    - *Note*: A practical guide to automating infrastructure with Terraform and VMware.
6. *Managing Kubernetes Performance at Scale*
    - *Note*: A guide to operating and tuning Kubernetes performance in production.
