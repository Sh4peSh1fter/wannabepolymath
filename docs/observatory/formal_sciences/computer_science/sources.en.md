# Sources & Further Reading — Computer Science

A curated list of resources for diving deeper into computer science. Every entry names the
resource, links it, and says in one line why it earns a place here.

## Websites & Online Publications

1. The Odin Project
    - *Link*: [theodinproject.com](https://www.theodinproject.com/)
    - *Note*: A free, open-source full-stack web development curriculum built around projects.
2. MIT OpenCourseWare
    - *Link*: [ocw.mit.edu](https://ocw.mit.edu/)
    - *Note*: Free lecture notes, assignments, and exams from MIT's computer science courses.
3. GeeksforGeeks
    - *Link*: [geeksforgeeks.org](https://www.geeksforgeeks.org/)
    - *Note*: A broad reference for algorithms, data structures, and language-specific tutorials.
4. Stack Overflow
    - *Link*: [stackoverflow.com](https://stackoverflow.com/)
    - *Note*: The largest question-and-answer community for practical programming problems.

## Videos & Documentaries

1. CS50 — Harvard's Introduction to Computer Science
    - *Link*: [cs50.harvard.edu](https://cs50.harvard.edu/)
    - *Note*: A rigorous, widely followed introductory course spanning C, Python, SQL, and web development.
2. Computerphile
    - *Link*: [youtube.com/user/Computerphile](https://www.youtube.com/user/Computerphile)
    - *Note*: Short videos explaining computing concepts, often featuring university researchers.

## Academic Papers & Journals

1. Communications of the ACM
    - *Link*: [cacm.acm.org](https://cacm.acm.org/)
    - *Note*: The flagship magazine of the ACM, covering research and practice across computing.

## Books

1. *Structure and Interpretation of Computer Programs* — by Harold Abelson and Gerald Jay Sussman
    - *Note*: A classic introduction to programming and abstraction, long used in MIT's core curriculum.
2. *Introduction to Algorithms* — by Thomas H. Cormen, Charles E. Leiserson, Ronald L. Rivest, and Clifford Stein
    - *Note*: The standard comprehensive reference on algorithms and data structures ("CLRS").
3. *The Art of Computer Programming* — by Donald Knuth
    - *Note*: A multi-volume, deeply rigorous treatment of fundamental algorithms.
4. *The Pragmatic Programmer* — by Andrew Hunt and David Thomas
    - *Note*: A practical guide to software craftsmanship and professional development habits.
