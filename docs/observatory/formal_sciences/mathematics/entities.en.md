# Key Entities in Mathematics

A list of key individuals and organizations that have made significant contributions to Mathematics.

## Key Figures

 - Euclid
    - *Description* - Often referred to as the "founder of geometry" or the "father of geometry". His "Elements" is one of the most influential works in the history of mathematics.
    - *Link* - <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euclid>

 - Isaac Newton
    - *Description* - A key figure in the scientific revolution, he developed the principles of modern physics and is credited with the co-invention of calculus.
    - *Link* - <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaac_Newton>
