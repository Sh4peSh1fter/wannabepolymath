# Key Entities — Mathematics

Key people, organizations, and institutions that shaped mathematics. Entries are neutral and
descriptive — what they are and why they matter.

## Key Figures

- **Euclid**
    - *Also known as*: Euclid of Alexandria
    - *Description*: Ancient Greek mathematician, "father of geometry"; his *Elements* is one of the most influential works in the history of mathematics.
    - *Known for*: [Euclidean geometry](./keywords.en.md#geometry)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Euclid)

- **Isaac Newton**
    - *Description*: English polymath of the scientific revolution; co-inventor of calculus and author of the *Principia*.
    - *Known for*: [Calculus](./keywords.en.md#calculus-analysis)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Isaac_Newton)

## Influencers

## Organizations & Companies

- **American Mathematical Society**
    - *Also known as*: AMS
    - *Description*: Association of professional mathematicians advancing mathematical research and scholarship.
    - *Link*: [ams.org](https://www.ams.org)

## Academic & Research Institutions

- **Clay Mathematics Institute**
    - *Description*: Private non-profit foundation dedicated to increasing and disseminating mathematical knowledge; known for the Millennium Prize Problems.
    - *Link*: [claymath.org](https://www.claymath.org)
