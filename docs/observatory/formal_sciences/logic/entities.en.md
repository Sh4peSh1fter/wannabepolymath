# Key Entities — Logic

Key people and organizations that shaped logic. Entries are neutral and descriptive — what
they are and why they matter.

## Key Figures

- **Aristotle**
    - *Description*: Ancient Greek philosopher whose *Organon* founded formal logic and the theory of the syllogism.
    - *Known for*: [Syllogism](./keywords.en.md#proof-inference)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aristotle)

- **George Boole**
    - *Description*: English mathematician who recast logic as algebra, giving rise to Boolean algebra.
    - *Known for*: [Boolean algebra](./keywords.en.md#propositional-logic)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Boole)

- **Gottlob Frege**
    - *Description*: German logician whose *Begriffsschrift* introduced quantifiers and founded modern predicate logic.
    - *Known for*: [First-order logic](./keywords.en.md#predicate-logic)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gottlob_Frege)

- **Bertrand Russell**
    - *Description*: British philosopher and logician, co-author of *Principia Mathematica* and discoverer of the paradox that bears his name.
    - *Known for*: [Russell's paradox](./keywords.en.md#set-theory-foundations)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bertrand_Russell)

- **Kurt Gödel**
    - *Description*: Austrian-American logician whose incompleteness theorems reshaped the foundations of mathematics.
    - *Known for*: [Gödel's incompleteness theorems](./keywords.en.md#computability-metalogic)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kurt_G%C3%B6del)

- **Alfred Tarski**
    - *Description*: Polish-American logician known for his formal definition of truth and contributions to model theory.
    - *Known for*: [First-order logic](./keywords.en.md#predicate-logic)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alfred_Tarski)

- **Alan Turing**
    - *Description*: English mathematician who formalized computation with the Turing machine and settled the limits of the decidable.
    - *Known for*: [Turing machine](./keywords.en.md#computability-metalogic)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alan_Turing)

## Organizations & Companies

- **Association for Symbolic Logic**
    - *Also known as*: ASL
    - *Description*: International organization founded in 1936 that supports research and scholarship in symbolic logic.
    - *Link*: [aslonline.org](https://aslonline.org)
