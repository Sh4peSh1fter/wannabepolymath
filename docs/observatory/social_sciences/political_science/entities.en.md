# Key Entities — Political Science

Key people, organizations, and institutions that shaped political science. Entries are neutral
and descriptive — what they are and why they matter.

## Key Figures

- **Aristotle**
    - *Description*: Ancient Greek philosopher whose treatise *Politics* founded the systematic study of the state, citizenship, and forms of government.
    - *Known for*: [Political theory](./keywords.en.md#political-theory)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aristotle)

- **Niccolò Machiavelli**
    - *Description*: Italian Renaissance diplomat and writer whose treatise *The Prince* is a foundational text of modern political realism.
    - *Known for*: [Political theory](./keywords.en.md#political-theory)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Niccol%C3%B2_Machiavelli)

- **Thomas Hobbes**
    - *Description*: English philosopher whose *Leviathan* argued for a strong sovereign as the remedy to the "state of nature"; a key social-contract theorist.
    - *Known for*: [Social contract](./keywords.en.md#political-theory)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Hobbes)

- **John Locke**
    - *Description*: English philosopher, "father of liberalism," whose theory of natural rights and government by consent shaped modern constitutional thought.
    - *Known for*: [Liberalism](./keywords.en.md#political-ideologies)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/John_Locke)

- **Montesquieu**
    - *Also known as*: Charles-Louis de Secondat, Baron de Montesquieu
    - *Description*: French Enlightenment thinker whose *The Spirit of the Laws* articulated the doctrine of the separation of powers.
    - *Known for*: [Separation of powers](./keywords.en.md#political-theory)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Montesquieu)

- **Jean-Jacques Rousseau**
    - *Description*: Genevan philosopher of the Enlightenment whose *The Social Contract* developed the idea of popular sovereignty and the general will.
    - *Known for*: [Social contract](./keywords.en.md#political-theory)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Jacques_Rousseau)

- **Max Weber**
    - *Description*: German sociologist who defined the state through its monopoly on legitimate force and analyzed authority, legitimacy, and bureaucracy.
    - *Known for*: [Legitimacy](./keywords.en.md#political-theory) and [bureaucracy](./keywords.en.md#public-policy-administration)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Weber)

## Organizations & Companies

- **United Nations**
    - *Also known as*: UN
    - *Description*: Intergovernmental organization founded in 1945 to maintain international peace and security and foster cooperation among states.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/United_Nations)

- **American Political Science Association**
    - *Also known as*: APSA
    - *Description*: The leading professional organization for the study of political science in the United States.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Political_Science_Association)

## Academic & Research Institutions

- **London School of Economics and Political Science**
    - *Also known as*: LSE
    - *Description*: Public research university in London specializing in the social sciences, including political science and international relations.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/London_School_of_Economics)
