# Key Entities — Anthropology

Key people, organizations, and institutions that shaped anthropology. Entries are neutral and
descriptive — what they are and why they matter.

## Key Figures

- **Franz Boas**
    - *Description*: German-American anthropologist regarded as a founder of modern American anthropology; developed cultural relativism and the four-field approach.
    - *Known for*: [Cultural relativism](./keywords.en.md#cultural-anthropology)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Franz_Boas)

- **Bronisław Malinowski**
    - *Description*: Polish-British anthropologist and a pioneer of intensive ethnographic fieldwork and participant observation; associated with functionalism.
    - *Known for*: [Participant observation](./keywords.en.md#ethnography-fieldwork)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bronis%C5%82aw_Malinowski)

- **Margaret Mead**
    - *Description*: American cultural anthropologist known for her studies of culture and socialization in the South Pacific, including *Coming of Age in Samoa*.
    - *Known for*: [Cultural anthropology](./keywords.en.md#cultural-anthropology)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Margaret_Mead)

- **Claude Lévi-Strauss**
    - *Description*: French anthropologist and founder of structural anthropology, known for his analyses of kinship, myth, and classification.
    - *Known for*: [Kinship & social organization](./keywords.en.md#kinship-social-organization)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Claude_L%C3%A9vi-Strauss)

- **Clifford Geertz**
    - *Description*: American anthropologist and a leading figure in symbolic and interpretive anthropology, associated with the method of thick description.
    - *Known for*: [Thick description](./keywords.en.md#ethnography-fieldwork)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clifford_Geertz)

- **Ruth Benedict**
    - *Description*: American anthropologist and student of Franz Boas, known for her comparative study of cultures in *Patterns of Culture*.
    - *Known for*: [Cultural anthropology](./keywords.en.md#cultural-anthropology)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ruth_Benedict)

## Organizations & Companies

- **American Anthropological Association**
    - *Also known as*: AAA
    - *Description*: The largest professional organization of anthropologists, publishing journals and setting disciplinary standards.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/American_Anthropological_Association)

## Academic & Research Institutions

- **Royal Anthropological Institute of Great Britain and Ireland**
    - *Also known as*: RAI
    - *Description*: The United Kingdom's long-established scholarly body for the study of humankind, supporting research and publication in anthropology.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Anthropological_Institute_of_Great_Britain_and_Ireland)
