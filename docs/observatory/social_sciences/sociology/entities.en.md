# Key Entities — Sociology

Key people, organizations, and institutions that shaped sociology. Entries are neutral and
descriptive — what they are and why they matter.

## Key Figures

- **Auguste Comte**
    - *Description*: French philosopher who coined the term "sociology" and founded positivism, arguing that society could be studied through the scientific method.
    - *Known for*: [Social theory](./keywords.en.md#social-theory)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Auguste_Comte)

- **Karl Marx**
    - *Description*: German philosopher and economist whose analysis of class conflict and capitalism became foundational to conflict theory.
    - *Known for*: [Conflict theory](./keywords.en.md#social-theory)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karl_Marx)

- **Émile Durkheim**
    - *Description*: French sociologist who helped establish sociology as an academic discipline; studied social solidarity, the division of labor, anomie, and suicide.
    - *Known for*: [Social solidarity](./keywords.en.md#social-structure-institutions)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/%C3%89mile_Durkheim)

- **Max Weber**
    - *Description*: German sociologist whose work on bureaucracy, authority, ideal types, and the relationship between religion and capitalism shaped modern social science.
    - *Known for*: [Bureaucracy](./keywords.en.md#social-structure-institutions)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Max_Weber)

- **W. E. B. Du Bois**
    - *Description*: American sociologist and civil-rights activist; a pioneer of empirical social research and of the sociological study of race and inequality.
    - *Known for*: [Stratification & inequality](./keywords.en.md#stratification-inequality)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W._E._B._Du_Bois)

- **Pierre Bourdieu**
    - *Description*: French sociologist known for theories of cultural capital, habitus, and social reproduction of inequality.
    - *Known for*: [Cultural capital](./keywords.en.md#stratification-inequality)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pierre_Bourdieu)

## Organizations & Associations

- **American Sociological Association**
    - *Also known as*: ASA
    - *Description*: Professional association dedicated to advancing sociology as a scientific discipline and profession; publishes leading journals.
    - *Link*: [asanet.org](https://www.asanet.org)

- **International Sociological Association**
    - *Also known as*: ISA
    - *Description*: Worldwide association of sociologists, affiliated with UNESCO, promoting sociological research and scholarly exchange across countries.
    - *Link*: [isa-sociology.org](https://www.isa-sociology.org)
