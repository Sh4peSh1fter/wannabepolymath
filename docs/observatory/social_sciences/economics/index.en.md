# Economics

## Introduction

Economics is the social science that studies how individuals, firms, governments, and nations
produce, distribute, and consume goods and services, and how they allocate scarce resources
that have alternative uses. It spans the small-scale decisions of buyers and sellers
(microeconomics) and the behavior of whole economies (macroeconomics), and it grounds the
everyday practice of saving and investing. This field is organized in three views: the working
**vocabulary**, the **people and institutions** behind it, and where to **read further**.

## Related concepts & fields

- **[Political Science](../political_science/index.en.md)** — fiscal and monetary policy,
  regulation, and public spending sit at the boundary of economics and government.
- **[Sociology](../sociology/index.en.md)** — markets are embedded in social structures, and
  both fields study how groups make and are shaped by collective choices.
- **[History](../history/index.en.md)** — economic history explains how institutions, trade,
  and crises shaped today's economies.
- **[Mathematics](../../formal_sciences/mathematics/index.en.md)** — probability, calculus,
  and statistics supply the tools for economic modeling, optimization, and finance.
