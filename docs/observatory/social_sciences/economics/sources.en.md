## Academic Papers & Journals

1. National Bureau of Economic Research (NBER)
    - *Link*: [nber.org](https://www.nber.org/)
    - *Note*: Leading U.S. nonprofit that publishes economic research working papers.
2. American Economic Review
    - *Link*: [aeaweb.org/journals/aer](https://www.aeaweb.org/journals/aer)
    - *Note*: Flagship peer-reviewed journal of the American Economic Association.

## Books

1. *The Wealth of Nations* — by Adam Smith
    - *Note*: The 1776 foundational work of classical economics.
2. *The Little Book of Common Sense Investing* — by John C. Bogle
    - *Note*: The case for low-cost index-fund investing, by the founder of Vanguard.
3. *Capital in the Twenty-First Century* — by Thomas Piketty
    - *Note*: A widely discussed analysis of wealth and income inequality.
