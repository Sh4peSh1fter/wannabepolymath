# Sources & Further Reading — Psychology

A curated list of resources for diving deeper into psychology.

## Websites & Online Publications

1. Simply Psychology
    - *Link*: [simplypsychology.org](https://www.simplypsychology.org)
    - *Note*: Accessible explainers of major theories, classic studies, and key figures.
2. APA Dictionary of Psychology
    - *Link*: [dictionary.apa.org](https://dictionary.apa.org)
    - *Note*: Authoritative reference definitions maintained by the American Psychological Association.

## Videos & Documentaries

1. TED — Psychology
    - *Link*: [ted.com/topics/psychology](https://www.ted.com/topics/psychology)
    - *Note*: A curated collection of talks on mind, behavior, and decision-making.

## Academic Papers & Journals

1. *Psychological Review*
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Psychological_Review)
    - *Note*: The flagship theoretical journal of the APA, publishing since 1894.

## Books

1. *Thinking, Fast and Slow* — by Daniel Kahneman
    - *Note*: A synthesis of decades of research on the two systems that drive how we think and decide.
2. *Man's Search for Meaning* — by Viktor Frankl
    - *Note*: A psychiatrist's account of finding purpose in suffering, and the basis of logotherapy.
3. *The Principles of Psychology* — by William James
    - *Note*: The foundational 1890 text that helped establish psychology as a modern discipline.
