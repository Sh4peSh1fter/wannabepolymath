# Key Entities — Psychology

Key people, organizations, and institutions that shaped psychology. Entries are neutral and
descriptive — what they are and why they matter.

## Key Figures

- **Wilhelm Wundt**
    - *Description*: German physiologist who founded the first formal laboratory for psychological research (Leipzig, 1879), establishing psychology as an experimental science.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wilhelm_Wundt)

- **William James**
    - *Description*: American philosopher and psychologist, often called the "father of American psychology"; author of *The Principles of Psychology*.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_James)

- **Ivan Pavlov**
    - *Description*: Russian physiologist whose work on classical conditioning in dogs became foundational to behaviorism.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ivan_Pavlov)

- **Sigmund Freud**
    - *Description*: Austrian neurologist and founder of psychoanalysis, a theory and clinical method for treating psychopathology through dialogue.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigmund_Freud)

- **Jean Piaget**
    - *Description*: Swiss psychologist known for his pioneering theory of cognitive development in children.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean_Piaget)

- **B. F. Skinner**
    - *Description*: American psychologist and leading figure of radical behaviorism; developed the theory of operant conditioning.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/B._F._Skinner)

- **Carl Rogers**
    - *Description*: American psychologist and one of the founders of the humanistic approach to psychology.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carl_Rogers)

- **Daniel Kahneman**
    - *Description*: Israeli-American psychologist awarded the 2002 Nobel Memorial Prize in Economic Sciences for work on judgment and decision-making under uncertainty.
    - *Known for*: [Judgment & Decision-Making](./keywords.en.md#judgment-decision-making)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daniel_Kahneman)

- **Amos Tversky**
    - *Description*: Israeli cognitive and mathematical psychologist; longtime collaborator with Daniel Kahneman on heuristics and cognitive biases.
    - *Known for*: [Cognitive Psychology](./keywords.en.md#cognitive-psychology)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amos_Tversky)

- **Ian Deary**
    - *Description*: Scottish psychologist known for research on human intelligence, cognitive ageing, and individual differences.
    - *Known for*: [Psychometrics](./keywords.en.md#psychometrics)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ian_Deary)

## Organizations & Companies

- **American Psychological Association**
    - *Also known as*: APA
    - *Description*: The largest scientific and professional organization of psychologists in the United States.
    - *Link*: [apa.org](https://www.apa.org)

- **Association for Psychological Science**
    - *Also known as*: APS
    - *Description*: International non-profit dedicated to the advancement of scientific psychology and its representation across sub-disciplines.
    - *Link*: [psychologicalscience.org](https://www.psychologicalscience.org)

- **British Psychological Society**
    - *Also known as*: BPS
    - *Description*: The representative body for psychology and psychologists in the United Kingdom, responsible for professional standards.
    - *Link*: [bps.org.uk](https://www.bps.org.uk)
