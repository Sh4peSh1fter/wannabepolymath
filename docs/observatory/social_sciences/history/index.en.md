# History

## Introduction

History is the study of the past — using sources and narrative to describe, examine, and
explain past events and their patterns of cause and effect. This page is a **Map of Content**:
it orients you to the field and links out to the details. The discipline turns on a few core
questions — what happened, how do we know, and how should the past be divided and interpreted?
Those questions run from the practice of reading and criticizing **sources**, through schemes
of **periodization**, to the broad eras of **ancient**, **medieval**, and **modern** history,
and finally to **historiography**, the study of how history itself is written.

## Related concepts & fields

- **[Anthropology](../anthropology/index.en.md)** — shares the study of human societies over
  time; archaeology and material culture supply much of the evidence for early history.
- **[Political Science](../political_science/index.en.md)** — the histories of states,
  revolutions, and institutions are common ground for both fields.
- **[Philosophy](../../humanities/philosophy/index.en.md)** — the philosophy of history asks
  what historical explanation, causation, and objectivity actually mean.
- **[Military](../../other/military/index.en.md)** — military history is one of the oldest
  branches of the discipline, from Thucydides onward.
