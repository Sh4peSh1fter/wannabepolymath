# Key Entities — History

Key people, organizations, and institutions that shaped the discipline of history. Entries are
neutral and descriptive — what they are and why they matter.

## Key Figures

- **Herodotus**
    - *Also known as*: the "Father of History"
    - *Description*: Ancient Greek writer whose *Histories* is often regarded as the founding work of the Western historical tradition.
    - *Known for*: [Historiography](./keywords.en.md#historiography)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Herodotus)

- **Thucydides**
    - *Description*: Ancient Greek historian of the *History of the Peloponnesian War*, noted for his emphasis on evidence and cause rather than myth.
    - *Known for*: [Historical method](./keywords.en.md#historical-methods)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thucydides)

- **Sima Qian**
    - *Description*: Han-dynasty historian regarded as the father of Chinese historiography; author of the *Records of the Grand Historian*.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sima_Qian)

- **Ibn Khaldun**
    - *Description*: 14th-century North African historian whose *Muqaddimah* pioneered the analytical study of social change and is seen as a forerunner of historiography and sociology.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ibn_Khaldun)

- **Edward Gibbon**
    - *Description*: English historian whose *The History of the Decline and Fall of the Roman Empire* set a model for narrative history grounded in primary sources.
    - *Known for*: [Ancient history](./keywords.en.md#ancient-history)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Edward_Gibbon)

- **Leopold von Ranke**
    - *Description*: German historian who established source-based, archival history as a professional discipline, urging historians to show the past "as it actually was".
    - *Known for*: [Historical method](./keywords.en.md#historical-methods)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leopold_von_Ranke)

- **Fernand Braudel**
    - *Description*: Leading figure of the Annales school, known for studying history over the *longue durée* — slow-moving geographic and economic structures.
    - *Known for*: [Annales school](./keywords.en.md#historiography)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fernand_Braudel)

## Influencers

- **Dan Carlin**
    - *Description*: Broadcaster whose *Hardcore History* podcast presents deeply researched, long-form narratives of past events to a wide audience.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dan_Carlin)

## Organizations & Companies

- **American Historical Association**
    - *Also known as*: AHA
    - *Description*: The largest professional association of historians in the United States, supporting research, teaching, and the preservation of history.
    - *Link*: [historians.org](https://www.historians.org)

- **Royal Historical Society**
    - *Also known as*: RHS
    - *Description*: A learned society founded in 1868 that promotes historical scholarship in the United Kingdom.
    - *Link*: [royalhistsoc.org](https://royalhistsoc.org)

## Academic & Research Institutions

- **Institute of Historical Research**
    - *Also known as*: IHR
    - *Description*: A research institute of the University of London providing library resources, seminars, and training for historians.
    - *Link*: [history.ac.uk](https://www.history.ac.uk)
