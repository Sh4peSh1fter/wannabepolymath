# Sources & Further Reading — History

A curated list of resources for diving deeper into history.

## Websites & Online Publications

1. World History Encyclopedia
    - *Link*: [worldhistory.org](https://www.worldhistory.org/)
    - *Note*: A peer-reviewed, freely accessible reference covering world history from prehistory onward.
2. Internet History Sourcebooks Project
    - *Link*: [sourcebooks.fordham.edu](https://sourcebooks.fordham.edu/)
    - *Note*: A large collection of public-domain primary-source texts, hosted by Fordham University.
3. BBC History
    - *Link*: [bbc.co.uk/history](https://www.bbc.co.uk/history)
    - *Note*: Accessible articles and features spanning ancient to modern history.

## Videos & Documentaries

1. Hardcore History
    - *Link*: [dancarlin.com](https://www.dancarlin.com/hardcore-history-series/)
    - *Note*: Long-form audio documentaries by Dan Carlin exploring major episodes of the past in depth.
2. Crash Course
    - *Link*: [youtube.com/@crashcourse](https://www.youtube.com/@crashcourse)
    - *Note*: Fast-paced educational series including multi-part courses on world and European history.

## Academic Papers & Journals

1. The American Historical Review
    - *Link*: [academic.oup.com/ahr](https://academic.oup.com/ahr)
    - *Note*: The flagship journal of the American Historical Association, covering all fields of history.
2. Past & Present
    - *Link*: [academic.oup.com/past](https://academic.oup.com/past)
    - *Note*: A leading journal of historical studies with an emphasis on social and cultural history.

## Books

1. *What Is History?* — by E. H. Carr
    - *Note*: A classic reflection on the nature of history and the historian's craft.
2. *The Landscape of History* — by John Lewis Gaddis
    - *Note*: An accessible introduction to how historians think and reason about the past.
3. *The Histories* — by Herodotus
    - *Note*: The foundational work of Western history, blending inquiry, ethnography, and narrative.
