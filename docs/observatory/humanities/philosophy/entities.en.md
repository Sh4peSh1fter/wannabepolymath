# Key Entities — Philosophy

Key people and organizations that shaped philosophy. Entries are neutral and descriptive —
what they are and why they matter.

## Key Figures

- **Socrates**
    - *Description*: Classical Greek philosopher credited as a founder of Western philosophy; known for the Socratic method of inquiry through questioning.
    - *Known for*: [Epistemology](./keywords.en.md#epistemology), [Ethics](./keywords.en.md#ethics)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Socrates)

- **Plato**
    - *Description*: Athenian philosopher, student of Socrates, and founder of the Academy; author of the *Republic* and originator of the theory of Forms.
    - *Known for*: [Metaphysics](./keywords.en.md#metaphysics)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Plato)

- **Aristotle**
    - *Description*: Greek philosopher and polymath, student of Plato; founder of formal logic and of virtue ethics.
    - *Known for*: [Logic](./keywords.en.md#logic)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aristotle)

- **René Descartes**
    - *Description*: French philosopher and mathematician often called the "father of modern philosophy"; advanced rationalism and mind–body dualism ("I think, therefore I am").
    - *Known for*: [Philosophy of Mind](./keywords.en.md#philosophy-of-mind)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ren%C3%A9_Descartes)

- **David Hume**
    - *Description*: Scottish Enlightenment philosopher known for a thoroughgoing empiricism and skepticism, especially about causation and the self.
    - *Known for*: [Epistemology](./keywords.en.md#epistemology)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/David_Hume)

- **Immanuel Kant**
    - *Description*: German Enlightenment philosopher whose critical philosophy reshaped ethics and epistemology; formulated the categorical imperative.
    - *Known for*: [Ethics](./keywords.en.md#ethics)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Immanuel_Kant)

- **Friedrich Nietzsche**
    - *Description*: German philosopher known for his critique of traditional morality and religion and for concepts such as the will to power and nihilism.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friedrich_Nietzsche)

- **Aldous Huxley**
    - *Description*: English writer and philosopher, author of the dystopian novel *Brave New World* and the essay *The Doors of Perception*.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aldous_Huxley)

## Organizations

- **American Philosophical Association**
    - *Also known as*: APA
    - *Description*: The principal professional organization of philosophers in the United States, promoting the discipline and the interests of professional philosophers.
    - *Link*: [apaonline.org](https://www.apaonline.org/)

- **Royal Institute of Philosophy**
    - *Description*: A UK learned society, founded in 1925, that promotes the study of philosophy and public engagement with it; publisher of the journal *Philosophy*.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Royal_Institute_of_Philosophy)
