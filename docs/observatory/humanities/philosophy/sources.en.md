# Sources & Further Reading — Philosophy

A curated list of resources for diving deeper into philosophy.

## Websites & Online Publications

1. Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy
    - *Link*: [plato.stanford.edu](https://plato.stanford.edu/)
    - *Note*: A peer-reviewed, continuously updated reference work with in-depth entries on virtually every philosophical topic.
2. Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy
    - *Link*: [iep.utm.edu](https://iep.utm.edu/)
    - *Note*: A peer-reviewed open-access encyclopedia of philosophy, often more accessible for newcomers.
3. PhilPapers
    - *Link*: [philpapers.org](https://philpapers.org/)
    - *Note*: A comprehensive index and bibliography of philosophy, cataloguing articles, books, and open-access papers.

## Videos & Documentaries

1. Crash Course Philosophy
    - *Link*: [youtube.com/@crashcourse](https://www.youtube.com/@crashcourse)
    - *Note*: An introductory video series on the CrashCourse channel covering core problems, thinkers, and branches of philosophy.

## Books

1. *A History of Western Philosophy* — by Bertrand Russell
    - *Note*: A sweeping single-author survey of Western philosophy from the pre-Socratics to the twentieth century.
2. *Sophie's World* — by Jostein Gaarder
    - *Note*: A novel that doubles as an accessible introduction to the history of philosophy.
3. *Republic* — by Plato
    - *Note*: A foundational dialogue on justice, the ideal state, and the theory of Forms.
4. *Nicomachean Ethics* — by Aristotle
    - *Note*: The classic statement of virtue ethics and the pursuit of eudaimonia (flourishing).
