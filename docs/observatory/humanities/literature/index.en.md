# Literature

## Introduction

Literature, in its broadest sense, is any collection of written work; more restrictively, it
is writing valued as an art form — poetry, drama, and prose fiction — and the study of how
such writing makes meaning. This field spans the **genres** writers work in, the **craft** of
narrative and verse, the **devices** that shape a text's effects, the **movements** that
define literary history, and the **criticism and theory** through which readers interpret it.
This page is a Map of Content: the definitions live in `keywords`, the writers and
institutions in `entities`, and the reading in `sources`.
