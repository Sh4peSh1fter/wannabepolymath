# Key Entities — Literature

Key people, organizations, and institutions that shaped literature. Entries are neutral and
descriptive — what they are and why they matter.

## Key Figures

- **Homer**
    - *Description*: Ancient Greek poet traditionally credited with the epic poems the *Iliad* and the *Odyssey*, foundational works of Western literature.
    - *Known for*: [Poetry](./keywords.en.md#literary-genres)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homer)

- **Aristotle**
    - *Description*: Ancient Greek philosopher whose *Poetics* is the earliest surviving work of dramatic and literary theory, introducing concepts such as plot, tragedy, and catharsis.
    - *Known for*: [Poetics](./keywords.en.md#narrative-poetics)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Aristotle)

- **William Shakespeare**
    - *Description*: English playwright and poet widely regarded as the greatest writer in the English language; author of works including *Hamlet*, *Macbeth*, and 154 sonnets.
    - *Known for*: [Drama](./keywords.en.md#literary-genres)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Shakespeare)

- **Leo Tolstoy**
    - *Description*: Russian novelist regarded as one of the greatest authors of realist fiction; author of *War and Peace* and *Anna Karenina*.
    - *Known for*: [Literary realism](./keywords.en.md#literary-movements)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leo_Tolstoy)

- **Virginia Woolf**
    - *Description*: English modernist writer, a pioneer of the stream-of-consciousness narrative mode; author of *Mrs Dalloway* and *To the Lighthouse*.
    - *Known for*: [Stream of consciousness](./keywords.en.md#narrative-poetics)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia_Woolf)

- **Northrop Frye**
    - *Description*: Canadian literary critic and theorist whose *Anatomy of Criticism* was among the most influential works of 20th-century literary theory.
    - *Known for*: [Literary criticism](./keywords.en.md#literary-criticism-theory)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northrop_Frye)

## Organizations & Institutions

- **Nobel Prize in Literature**
    - *Description*: Awarded annually by the Swedish Academy to an author for outstanding contributions in the field of literature; one of the most prestigious literary awards.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nobel_Prize_in_Literature)

- **Modern Language Association**
    - *Also known as*: MLA
    - *Description*: The principal professional association in the United States for scholars of language and literature; publisher of the widely used MLA style.
    - *Link*: [mla.org](https://www.mla.org)
