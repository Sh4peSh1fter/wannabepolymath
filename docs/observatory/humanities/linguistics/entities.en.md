# Key Entities — Linguistics

Key people, organizations, and institutions that shaped linguistics. Entries are neutral and
descriptive — what they are and why they matter.

## Key Figures

- **Pāṇini**
    - *Description*: Ancient Indian scholar whose *Aṣṭādhyāyī* gave a comprehensive, formal generative grammar of Sanskrit and is a foundational work of descriptive linguistics.
    - *Known for*: [Morphology](./keywords.en.md#morphology)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P%C4%81%E1%B9%87ini)

- **Ferdinand de Saussure**
    - *Description*: Swiss linguist often called the father of modern linguistics; his posthumous *Course in General Linguistics* founded structuralism and semiotics.
    - *Known for*: [Semiotics](./keywords.en.md#semantics-pragmatics)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ferdinand_de_Saussure)

- **Leonard Bloomfield**
    - *Description*: American linguist and a central figure of structural linguistics in the United States; his 1933 book *Language* shaped a generation of the field.
    - *Known for*: [Syntax](./keywords.en.md#syntax)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leonard_Bloomfield)

- **Roman Jakobson**
    - *Description*: Russian-American linguist central to structuralism and the study of the functions of language and communication.
    - *Known for*: [Phonology](./keywords.en.md#phonetics-phonology)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roman_Jakobson)

- **Noam Chomsky**
    - *Description*: American linguist whose theory of generative grammar and the concept of universal grammar transformed 20th-century linguistics.
    - *Known for*: [Generative grammar](./keywords.en.md#syntax)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Noam_Chomsky)

- **William Labov**
    - *Description*: American linguist widely regarded as the founder of variationist sociolinguistics, studying language change in relation to social factors.
    - *Known for*: [Sociolinguistics](./keywords.en.md#sociolinguistics)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/William_Labov)

## Organizations & Companies

- **Linguistic Society of America**
    - *Also known as*: LSA
    - *Description*: Learned society founded in 1924 to advance the scientific study of language; publisher of the journal *Language*.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linguistic_Society_of_America)

- **SIL International**
    - *Also known as*: Summer Institute of Linguistics
    - *Description*: Non-profit organization that studies, documents, and develops lesser-known languages; publisher of the *Ethnologue* catalog of world languages.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/SIL_International)
