# Sources & Further Reading — Linguistics

A curated list of resources for diving deeper into linguistics.

## Websites & Online Publications

1. Linguistic Society of America
    - *Link*: [linguisticsociety.org](https://www.linguisticsociety.org/)
    - *Note*: The main professional body for linguistics in the US, with accessible explainers, resources, and career information.
2. Ethnologue
    - *Link*: [ethnologue.com](https://www.ethnologue.com/)
    - *Note*: A reference catalog of the world's living languages, with data on speakers, families, and geographic distribution.
3. SIL Glossary of Linguistic Terms
    - *Link*: [glossary.sil.org](https://glossary.sil.org/)
    - *Note*: A searchable glossary defining terminology across the sub-fields of linguistics.

## Videos & Documentaries

1. The Ling Space
    - *Link*: [thelingspace.com](https://www.thelingspace.com/)
    - *Note*: Short, approachable video lessons introducing core concepts across the sub-fields of linguistics.
2. NativLang
    - *Link*: [youtube.com/c/NativLang](https://www.youtube.com/c/NativLang)
    - *Note*: Videos on language history, writing systems, and historical linguistics.

## Academic Papers & Journals

1. *Language* (journal of the Linguistic Society of America)
    - *Link*: [linguisticsociety.org/publications/language](https://www.linguisticsociety.org/publications/language)
    - *Note*: One of the flagship peer-reviewed journals of general linguistics.

## Books

1. *Course in General Linguistics* — by Ferdinand de Saussure
    - *Note*: The 1916 posthumous work that founded structural linguistics and modern semiotics.
2. *Language* — by Leonard Bloomfield
    - *Note*: A 1933 landmark of American structuralism that long served as a standard textbook.
3. *Syntactic Structures* — by Noam Chomsky
    - *Note*: The 1957 monograph that launched generative grammar and reshaped modern syntax.
4. *The Language Instinct* — by Steven Pinker
    - *Note*: A popular-science introduction arguing that language is an innate human faculty.
