# Sources & Further Reading — Medicine

A curated list of resources for diving deeper into medicine.

## Websites & Online Publications

1. MedlinePlus
    - *Link*: [medlineplus.gov](https://medlineplus.gov/)
    - *Note*: Consumer health information from the US National Library of Medicine, covering conditions, medications, and healthy living.
2. PubMed
    - *Link*: [pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/)
    - *Note*: A free search engine for biomedical literature, maintained by the US National Library of Medicine.

## Academic Papers & Journals

1. The New England Journal of Medicine
    - *Link*: [nejm.org](https://www.nejm.org/)
    - *Note*: A weekly medical journal published by the Massachusetts Medical Society; among the most prestigious peer-reviewed medical journals and the oldest continuously published one.
2. The Lancet
    - *Link*: [thelancet.com](https://www.thelancet.com/)
    - *Note*: One of the world's oldest and best-known general medical journals.

## Books

1. *How to Change Your Mind* — by Michael Pollan
    - *Note*: Explores the science and history of psychedelics and their potential in treating mental illness.
2. *Gray's Anatomy* — by Henry Gray
    - *Note*: A classic and enduring reference textbook of human anatomy, first published in 1858.
3. *Harrison's Principles of Internal Medicine*
    - *Note*: A leading reference textbook of internal medicine, widely used in medical education.
