# Medicine

## Introduction

Medicine is the science and practice of caring for patients — managing the diagnosis,
prognosis, prevention, treatment, and palliation of injury or disease, and promoting health.
It draws on the natural sciences (biology, chemistry) for its mechanisms and on the social
sciences for how care is delivered and how populations stay healthy. This field is organized
in three views: the working **vocabulary**, the **people and institutions** behind it, and
where to **read further**.
