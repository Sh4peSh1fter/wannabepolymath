# Sources & Further Reading — Engineering

A curated list of resources for diving deeper into engineering.

## Websites & Online Publications

1. MIT OpenCourseWare — Engineering
    - *Link*: [ocw.mit.edu](https://ocw.mit.edu/)
    - *Note*: Free lecture notes, assignments, and exams from MIT's engineering courses across all major disciplines.
2. The Engineering ToolBox
    - *Link*: [engineeringtoolbox.com](https://www.engineeringtoolbox.com/)
    - *Note*: A large reference of engineering data, formulas, and design tables for mechanical, civil, and process work.

## Videos & Documentaries

1. Practical Engineering
    - *Link*: [youtube.com/@PracticalEngineeringChannel](https://www.youtube.com/@PracticalEngineeringChannel)
    - *Note*: Civil engineer Grady Hillhouse explains infrastructure and how the built world works, often with hands-on demonstrations.

## Books

1. *Structures: Or Why Things Don't Fall Down* — by J. E. Gordon
    - *Note*: An accessible classic on why structures stand up (or fail), covering stress, strain, and materials.
2. *To Engineer Is Human: The Role of Failure in Successful Design* — by Henry Petroski
    - *Note*: Examines how engineering failures drive better design, using famous case studies.
3. *The Design of Everyday Things* — by Don Norman
    - *Note*: A foundational text on usable, human-centred design and how form should follow function.
