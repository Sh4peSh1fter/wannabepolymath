# Nutrition

## Introduction

Nutrition is the study of nutrients in food, how the body uses them, and the relationship
between diet, health, and disease. It examines how the body breaks food down for energy
(catabolism) and uses it to build and repair cells and tissue (anabolism), and how patterns
of eating shape long-term health. This page is a **Map of Content**: the working
**vocabulary**, the **people and institutions** behind the field, and where to **read further**.
