# Sources & Further Reading — Woodworking

A curated list of resources for diving deeper into woodworking.

## Websites & Online Publications

1. Fine Woodworking
    - *Link*: [finewoodworking.com](https://www.finewoodworking.com/)
    - *Note*: In-depth technique articles, project plans, and video workshops from a leading craft magazine.
2. Popular Woodworking
    - *Link*: [popularwoodworking.com](https://www.popularwoodworking.com/)
    - *Note*: Projects, tool reviews, and skill-building articles aimed at hobbyist and intermediate woodworkers.
3. The Wood Database
    - *Link*: [wood-database.com](https://www.wood-database.com/)
    - *Note*: A reference on hundreds of wood species, with properties, workability, and identification photos.

## Videos & Documentaries

1. The Wood Whisperer
    - *Link*: [thewoodwhisperer.com](https://www.thewoodwhisperer.com/)
    - *Note*: A large library of free video tutorials covering hand tools, power tools, joinery, and finishing.

## Books

1. *Understanding Wood: A Craftsman's Guide to Wood Technology* — by R. Bruce Hoadley
    - *Note*: A classic reference on wood as a material — structure, moisture, movement, and how it behaves under tools.
2. *The Complete Manual of Woodworking* — by Albert Jackson and David Day
    - *Note*: A broad, illustrated introduction covering tools, materials, joints, and techniques.
3. *A Cabinetmaker's Notebook* — by James Krenov
    - *Note*: A reflective, influential account of the fine-woodworking mindset and hand-tool craftsmanship.
4. *Tage Frid Teaches Woodworking* — by Tage Frid
    - *Note*: A three-volume series on joinery, shaping, and finishing that became a standard teaching text.
5. *The Anarchist's Tool Chest* — by Christopher Schwarz
    - *Note*: A widely read argument for a core kit of hand tools, with guidance on choosing and using them.
