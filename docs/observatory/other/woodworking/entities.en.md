# Key Entities — Woodworking

Key people and organizations that shaped woodworking. Entries are neutral and descriptive —
what they are and why they matter.

## Key Figures

- **André Jacob Roubo**
    - *Description*: 18th-century French cabinetmaker and author of *L'Art du menuisier*, one of the most comprehensive early treatises on the craft.
    - *Known for*: [Joinery](./keywords.en.md#joinery)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Andr%C3%A9_Jacob_Roubo)

- **Thomas Chippendale**
    - *Description*: English cabinet-maker whose pattern book *The Gentleman and Cabinet-Maker's Director* made his name a byword for a style of 18th-century furniture.
    - *Known for*: [Joinery](./keywords.en.md#joinery)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Thomas_Chippendale)

- **Gustav Stickley**
    - *Description*: American furniture manufacturer and a leading figure of the American Arts and Crafts (Mission) movement.
    - *Known for*: [Wood & Materials](./keywords.en.md#wood-materials)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gustav_Stickley)

- **Charles Hayward**
    - *Description*: British woodworker and long-time editor of *The Woodworker* magazine; a prolific author on hand-tool technique.
    - *Known for*: [Hand Tools](./keywords.en.md#hand-tools)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Hayward)

- **Tage Frid**
    - *Description*: Danish-American cabinetmaker and educator whose three-volume series taught a generation the fundamentals of joinery, shaping, and finishing.
    - *Known for*: [Joinery](./keywords.en.md#joinery)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tage_Frid)

- **James Krenov**
    - *Description*: Russian-born cabinetmaker, teacher, and author known for refined hand-tool furniture and for founding an influential fine-woodworking program.
    - *Known for*: [Hand Tools](./keywords.en.md#hand-tools)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/James_Krenov)

- **George Nakashima**
    - *Description*: American woodworker and architect who built furniture that celebrates the natural forms and grain of the wood.
    - *Known for*: [Wood & Materials](./keywords.en.md#wood-materials)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/George_Nakashima)

- **Sam Maloof**
    - *Description*: American furniture designer and woodworker celebrated for his hand-crafted rocking chairs and sculpted joinery.
    - *Known for*: [Joinery](./keywords.en.md#joinery)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sam_Maloof)

## Influencers

- **Roy Underhill**
    - *Description*: Host of the long-running public-television program *The Woodwright's Shop*, an advocate of traditional hand-tool woodworking.
    - *Known for*: [Hand Tools](./keywords.en.md#hand-tools)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Roy_Underhill)

- **Norm Abram**
    - *Description*: Master carpenter of *The New Yankee Workshop* who popularized power-tool woodworking for a broad television audience.
    - *Known for*: [Power Tools](./keywords.en.md#power-tools)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Norm_Abram)

- **Nick Offerman**
    - *Description*: Actor and woodworker who runs Offerman Woodshop and has authored popular books on the craft.
    - *Known for*: [Techniques & Operations](./keywords.en.md#techniques-operations)
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nick_Offerman)

## Organizations & Companies

- **Fine Woodworking**
    - *Description*: Long-running American magazine (published by The Taunton Press) offering in-depth technique articles, plans, and videos for woodworkers.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fine_Woodworking)

- **Popular Woodworking**
    - *Description*: American woodworking magazine and media brand aimed at hobbyist and intermediate woodworkers.
    - *Link*: [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Popular_Woodworking)
