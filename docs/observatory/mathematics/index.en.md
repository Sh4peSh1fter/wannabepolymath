# Mathematics

## Introduction

Mathematics is the study of topics such as quantity (numbers), structure, space, and change. It is an essential tool for understanding the world around us, from the laws of physics to the patterns of financial markets. This section of the Observatory is dedicated to exploring the core concepts, influential figures, and key resources in the vast field of mathematics.
