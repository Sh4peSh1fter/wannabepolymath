# Troubleshooting Journals

Troubleshooting journals are **problem→resolution logs**: a specific thing broke, and here's the
symptom, the investigation trail (dead ends included), the root cause, and the fix. They're a
how-to/reference hybrid — titled by the symptom, so the next person who hits the same wall can find
them.
