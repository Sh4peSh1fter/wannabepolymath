# Explainers

Explainers are **understanding-oriented deep dives**. They answer *why*, weigh trade-offs, and put
ideas in context — the kind of reading you do away from the keyboard. They are deliberately *not*
step-by-step instructions (see [tutorials](../tutorials/index.en.md) and
[how-to guides](../how_to_guides/index.en.md)) and *not* dry fact lists (that's the
[Observatory](../../observatory/index.en.md)). This is the fourth Diátaxis mode the Academy needs to
be complete.
