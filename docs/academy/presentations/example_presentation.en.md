## Abstract

A 20-minute tour of regular expressions for people who've always found them intimidating: what the
symbols mean, how to build a pattern piece by piece, and when a regex is the *wrong* tool. It mirrors
the [Regular Expressions from Scratch](../courses/example_course/index.en.md) course.
