## Key Accomplishments

*   **1905**: Published four groundbreaking papers, including on the photoelectric effect and special relativity (his "annus mirabilis").
*   **1915**: Completed his theory of general relativity.
*   **1921**: Awarded the Nobel Prize in Physics for his explanation of the photoelectric effect.
