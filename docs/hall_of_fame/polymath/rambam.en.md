## Key Accomplishments

*   **c. 1170-1180**: Authored the *Mishneh Torah*, a comprehensive and systematic code of Jewish law.
*   **c. 1190**: Completed the *Guide for the Perplexed*, a philosophical masterpiece that sought to reconcile Aristotelian philosophy with Jewish theology.
*   **Physician**: Served as the personal physician to the Sultan Saladin and his court in Egypt, and wrote numerous influential medical texts.
