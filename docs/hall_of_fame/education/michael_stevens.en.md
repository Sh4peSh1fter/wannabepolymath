## Key Accomplishments

A list of major achievements, projects, or ideas.

*   **2010**: Launched the *Vsauce* YouTube channel, which has grown to over 24 million subscribers.
*   **2013**: Presented two TED talks, "How much does a video weigh?" and "Why do we ask questions?".
*   **2017**: Created and hosted the YouTube Premium series *Mind Field*, exploring various aspects of human behavior.
*   **2017**: Toured with Adam Savage for the science-based stage show *Brain Candy Live!*.
*   **Multiple Years**: Won several Webby and Streamy Awards for science and education content.
