## Key Accomplishments

*   **1999**: Released his debut solo album *Operation: Doomsday*, which became an underground classic.
*   **2004**: Released two critically acclaimed albums, *Madvillainy* (with Madlib) and *Mm..Food*, cementing his legendary status.
*   **Influence**: Became one of the most celebrated and respected lyricists in hip-hop, known for his multi-syllabic rhymes and complex flows.
